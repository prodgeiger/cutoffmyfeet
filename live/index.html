🎬 Hi!! Welcome!!

If you are reading this, you typed cutoffmyfeet.net/live into your browser. Either you are very curious or somebody told you. Either way: hi. This is where I keep my notes on CUT OFF MY FEET, my capstone short film.

Everything on the main site is part of the movie. Mikey isn't real. The website he made is based on one that was. In 2001, a guy put up a site promising to cut off his own feet on a live webcast if people paid for his prosthetic legs. I couldn't stop thinking about it.

📝 What is this movie?

A short film about the morning of a broadcast that was supposed to change one man's life. A garage, a homemade guillotine, a dial-up connection, and a few people who all want something different from the same moment.

It's a time capsule. The early internet was weird and sincere and kind of terrifying, and nobody knew what any of it meant yet. That's the feeling I'm chasing.
